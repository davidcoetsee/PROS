# PROS trademark policy

This policy describes permitted uses of the PROS name and visual identity. It
is intended to let people discuss, share, and build on PROS while making it
clear which releases come from the PROS project. It does not claim that any
mark is registered, and it does not limit rights that applicable law permits
without a trademark licence.

## Marks covered

The policy covers the name **PROS**, the official PROS wordmark, the PROS logo,
the PROS application icon, and confusingly similar names or visual identities
used for PDF-processing software. These project marks are owned by David
Coetsee, the PROS project owner.

## Uses that do not require permission

You may:

- refer truthfully to PROS in reviews, tutorials, support material, issue
  reports, comparisons, and academic or news coverage;
- link to the PROS project or reproduce screenshots that accurately show the
  software;
- say that another product is compatible with PROS or is based on PROS,
  provided the statement is accurate and does not imply endorsement;
- redistribute an exact, unmodified official PROS release under its original
  name and branding, provided you identify its source and version, preserve
  the applicable notices, and do not suggest that you produced or sponsor the
  release; and
- use the name descriptively in a fork statement such as "ExamplePDF, based on
  PROS" or "an unofficial fork of PROS."

## Modified versions and forks

A modified version must use a distinct product name, logo, and application
icon. It must state prominently that it is unofficial, identify the project
or people responsible for the modifications, and avoid any presentation that
could reasonably be mistaken for an official PROS release.

You may use PROS descriptively to explain the software's origin, but do not use
PROS by itself, or a confusing variation of it, as the product name of a
modified release. Replacing the brand files does not affect your rights to use,
modify, or distribute the MPL-covered source code.

## Uses that need permission

Please ask the project maintainers before using a covered mark in a way that
could imply official status, sponsorship, or endorsement. Examples include:

- naming a modified product, service, business, domain, social-media account,
  or package so that it appears to be an official PROS offering;
- using the official logo or application icon as the primary branding for a
  modified release; or
- combining a covered mark with another mark to create a confusingly similar
  identity.

Requests can be made by opening an issue in the official repository at
<https://github.com/davidcoetsee/PROS/issues>. Permission is not needed for
truthful nominative, comparative, or otherwise legally permitted use.
